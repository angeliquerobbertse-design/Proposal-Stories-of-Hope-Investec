Roots · The partnership proposition

A year-long platform for the people building better futures

The National invites Investec Bank to become the founding and exclusive banking partner of a year-long platform profiling the philanthropists, foundations, community leaders and corporate social investment programmes helping South Africans build better futures.

The purpose is to turn credible storytelling into practical support for work that delivers measurable change.

Every month

Each month, a substantial profile will appear across print, online and social media.

Twelve flagship profiles give each story time to be researched, checked and followed up.

Every week

Weekly digital activity supports every monthly profile.

Profile extracts, interviews, community nomination invitations, project updates and calls for support. Platform mix and post counts are agreed at contracting.

Across the year

A community nomination programme will invite residents to write in about people making a difference where they live.

At least six of the twelve flagship profiles are reserved for verified community nominations.

At year end

The year will culminate in an impact review event and a commemorative supplement.

A premium coffee table book is available as an expanded option.

Stem · Community Champions

Who is bringing hope to your community?

Write to The National about someone making a difference. Tell us what they do, whose lives are changing and what could help them do more.

The community participation strand of Stories of Hope will invite South Africans to nominate the people and organisations improving life in their neighbourhoods. Residents become contributors to the national story, helping bring overlooked work to the attention of readers, potential funders and partners.

How communities will take part

A dedicated email addressAn online nomination formA managed WhatsApp channel

Promoted through print QR codes and social media. The National will establish and publish the contact details before launch. Written submissions, photographs and short voice notes will be accepted, with language assistance where practical. Entry will be free and no bank account or customer relationship will be required.

A good nomination includes

  • Person or organisation, community and province
  • The work and how long it has operated
  • A concrete example of change
  • A contactable reference

Try the form on the phone. It is illustrative only. Nominees will be approached for consent before any personal information or story is published.

Stem · A fair route from nomination to profile

Selected on merit, never on votes

1

Acknowledge

The National will acknowledge submissions.

Every nomination is received and acknowledged.

2

Screen monthly

Nominations are screened monthly.

A regular rhythm keeps the route fair and the queue moving.

3

Check the story

Shortlisted stories are checked.

Through interviews, references and available project records.

4

Select

Selection considers four things.

Demonstrable community benefit, sustained contribution, local credibility and potential for further development.

Within the 12-profile programme

Reserved for verified community nominations (at least six)Established philanthropists, foundations and CSI initiatives

Illustrative spread. The reserved profiles are part of the agreed total, and coverage will seek a meaningful spread of provinces and communities over the year.

The rule that protects the processThe number of nominations or social media votes will not determine selection.

Stem · Recognition that helps communities develop

One practical need, followed through

Each selected Community Champion will work with the reporting team to identify one practical development need.

Mentor supportLearning materialsEquipmentOrganisational skillsAccess to partners

The published profile will explain the need and direct willing supporters to an appropriate contact or recipient channel.

The National will facilitate relevant introductions. Investec may contribute staff expertise, client connections or approved programme referrals where appropriate. A separately funded community support or matching contribution option can be agreed with Investec; nomination and publication will not promise a grant.

Follow-up, allowing for publication date

1

Publication

The profile publishes, with the need clearly stated.

The reporting team and the Champion have agreed one practical development need, and the profile points supporters to where they can help.

2

About 3 months

Revisit after about three months.

Selected projects are revisited to record support received and resulting progress. Did a tutoring programme retain more learners?

3

About 6 months

Revisit again after about six months.

Updates can show whether a garden improved its production, or a youth enterprise gained customers or skills. The annual review includes community voices and recognises verified local contributions.

Bloom · The year in review

Where the year comes together

The event · A Celebration of Impact

Profiled philanthropists, Community Champions, community representatives, Investec clients and employees, foundations and potential funders come together to review progress, exchange practical lessons and secure commitments for the following year.

A short impact filmSelected project updatesA discussion on effective givingFacilitated introductions around specific needs

Commitments will record an owner, an intended contribution and a follow-up date. The event will recognise contribution without implying a ranked awards process.

The supplement · A Year of South Africans Making a Difference

A print and digital supplement draws together every profile, community contributions, follow-up outcomes and practical lessons.

Premium option · The coffee table book

A coffee table book would offer a premium photographic record, with links to current project updates.

It can replace or extend the supplement under a separately agreed scope. Page count, print run, distribution and image rights will be agreed within the chosen budget.

Bloom · How success will be measured

Evidence, reported honestly

Audience engagement

Delivered, read, referred

Delivered print placements; online users, engaged reading and referral clicks; social reach and engagement reported separately to avoid inflated combined reach.

Participation

Nominations to commitments

Nominations received and verified, geographic spread and community profiles published; qualified enquiries, introductions and supporter commitments linked to each profile.

Resources mobilised

Pledged versus received

Funding pledged versus received; skills hours and in-kind support confirmed by recipients. Existing project funding is distinguished from campaign-linked support.

Community progress

Baseline, period, source

A relevant project measure such as learner progression, training completion or jobs sustained, with baseline, reporting period and source. Wider results are not attributed solely to media exposure.

Investec value

Opt-in and scoped

Opt-in stakeholder engagement and a scoped brand association survey where funded. No guaranteed client acquisition or financial return.
